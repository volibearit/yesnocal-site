Privacy Policy

Effective: February 26, 2026 · Last updated: November 18, 2026
Everything you enter stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Only if you allow AI features, the app sends what they need to an AI provider. The built-in AI is Google's free Gemini, reached through a small YesNoCal server that keeps nothing but an anonymous daily count.

1. Who We Are

YesNoCal is an AI-powered nutrition tracking app made in Montréal, Québec, Canada by the developer shown as the seller on YesNoCal's App Store and Google Play pages ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information. It applies to the YesNoCal app and this website.

2. Data That Stays On Your Phone

YesNoCal is local-first. These are stored only on your device: meal logs and nutrition data, body details and weight history, supplements and doses, water, your plan, targets and tracked nutrients, badges and streaks, app settings, your name (for display only), and any AI keys you add (stored encrypted). We don't have a copy.

3. AI Features (only with your permission)

The app asks for your permission before anything is sent to an AI. You can withdraw it anytime in Settings → AI data sharing.

  • What is sent: what you type or say about a meal, and meal photos you take or pick. When you use the AI chat or ask about a meal or a day, the app also sends your profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity, goal, but not your name), your plan and targets, your meal history, weigh-ins, water, supplements, streaks and badges, so the AI can answer about you. Supplement reminders and celebrations may send your supplement names to write a short funny line.
  • Built-in AI (default): requests go from your phone to a YesNoCal server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards them to Google's Gemini API and sends back the answer. The server does not store meals, photos or questions. It keeps only a count of requests per anonymous install ID (a random code made by the app, not linked to you) to apply the free daily limit.
  • Google's free tier: the built-in AI uses Google's free Gemini service. Under Google's terms for free use, Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. Please don't include anything you wouldn't want reviewed. To avoid this, add your own paid AI key in Settings → AI engine.
  • Your own AI key (optional): requests go straight from your phone to the provider you chose: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini). That provider's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Where: AI providers and Cloudflare may process data outside your country, including in the United States.
  • Purpose: only to estimate nutrition and to answer your questions. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

Barcode lookups: when you photograph a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org). No personal information is included.

Voice: speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition (Apple or Google), under their terms.

4. What We Do Not Collect

No account or email, no location, no analytics or usage tracking, no advertising identifiers, no contacts. We don't sell or share data for advertising.

5. Device Permissions

Camera: meal photos and barcodes. Photo Library: pick an existing meal photo. Microphone and Speech Recognition: voice logging and the AI chat. Notifications: supplement reminders, meal nudges and weekly summaries (each can be switched off in the app).

6. Export, Backup and Deletion

Make a backup file in Settings → Backup & restore, and export your meals as CSV in History. "Reset everything" in Settings deletes all your data on the phone; uninstalling the app does the same. Because we don't hold your data, there's nothing on our side to delete.

7. How Long Data Is Kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, use "Reset everything", or uninstall the app.
  • YesNoCal server: no meals, photos or questions are kept. The only record is today's request count for your anonymous install ID; it is replaced every day and holds no history.
  • AI providers: according to their own terms. For Google's free Gemini service this may include keeping data to improve its products.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You can see, correct and delete everything YesNoCal holds about you directly in the app, because it is all on your phone. You can withdraw consent for AI features at any time (Settings → AI data sharing); the app then sends nothing to any AI provider. Depending on where you live (for example Québec, the rest of Canada, the EU or US states), you may have further rights, such as asking what we hold or filing a complaint with a privacy authority. In Québec that is the Commission d'accès à l'information. Contact us first at the address below and we'll answer within 30 days.

9. Health Information

What you enter (meals, weight, supplements, goals) is health information and is treated as sensitive. We only send it anywhere with your express consent, only for the purpose you used it for (estimating meals, answering your questions), and we never sell it, use it for advertising, or share it for any other purpose. Consumer health data (US, including Washington and Nevada): the categories are listed in section 3; they are used only to provide the AI features you ask for; they are shared only with the AI provider you use (Google, or the provider of your own key) and Cloudflare (which hosts our server), and only with your consent. You can withdraw consent or delete your data anytime as described in section 8.

10. Children

YesNoCal is for people aged 13 and older; if you're under 14 (or under the age of majority where you live), a parent or guardian must agree to your use. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Calorie and weight tracking isn't right for everyone; if you have or have had an eating disorder, please talk to a health professional before using it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes are shown in the app, and you'll be asked again before AI features use your data in a new way.

12. Contact and Privacy Officer

Our person in charge of the protection of personal information is our Privacy Officer, reachable at privacy@yesnocal.com. You can ask questions, withdraw consent or make a complaint there.
